## Arena artwork

The colosseum floor, the robot and human poses, the portraits and the cash chest in `web/assets/*.png` were generated on 2026-10-01 with an AI image tool (an OpenAI image model inside the Codex app). The prompts are kept next to the images (`web/assets/*prompt*`). No third-party characters or brands were requested. To the extent the authors hold any rights in these images, they are released under the MIT License of this repository.

## The "IKIGAI Triangle" strategy

The human-authored G1 strategy (`trading_lab/ikigai_g1*.py`, `strategies/myhuman-g1-ikigai-triangle/`) is a geometric adaptation inspired by public videos of a trading educator. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by that educator, and it reproduces no course material.
