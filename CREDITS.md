# Credits

AI Trading Arena was built by **[kalambyrYura](https://github.com/kalambyrYura)**, who set the goals and budgets and performed every action that moved money, together with three AI teams. The full story is in [docs/AI_TEAM.md](docs/AI_TEAM.md).

## Team 1: OpenAI Codex and GPT agents (29 Sep – 27 Oct 2026)

The largest team: about 28 GPT-based agents, chats and helper roles.

- **OpenAI Codex** ([@codex](https://github.com/codex)), the lead. It built the platform: the first paper prototype with the Jev gate, OKX connectivity and the one-trade executors, the PAPER league, the strategy workshop, the OKX Demo and Live engine, the colosseum viewer, the declarative V8 strategies with review, league and replay, the rules-only mode, the Windows server bundle and the P0 safety and causality fixes.
- **Sol** (`gpt-6.1-sol`) and **Astra** (`gpt-6-astra`), the first strategy authors.
- **UX and UI design chats** (started on `gpt-6-luna`), two research chats, about ten helper sub-agents.
- **A strategy-discovery team of ten Codex chats**: six authors, two critics, an evaluator and an intake analyst.
- **ChatGPT**, consulted through the owner: its specification drove the P0 safety and causality fixes.

## Team 2: a Claude team on a second account (1–3 Oct 2026)

- A **Claude coordinator** with two authors, **Opus** (Claude Opus 5.5) and **Sonnet** (Claude Sonnet 5.5), in Claude Cowork and then Claude Code.

## Team 3: a Claude Code team (28 Oct – 17 Nov 2026)

- Eleven sessions with fixed roles: a lead, a coordinator, a platform engineer, four strategy authors (the personas Astra, Sol, Sonnet and Opus), an assistant for the owner's own strategy slot (MYHuman), a reviewer, a judge and an operator of the trading PC.
